## Deviations and follow-ups

- `Header.tsx`, `Hero.tsx` and `crossfadeKeyframes.ts` are used by the site shell (T009/T011), so they were kept. Their `coming-soon-*` classes were renamed: `coming-soon-header` merged into `site-header`, `coming-soon-brand` → `site-brand`, `coming-soon-background` → `hero-background`, `coming-soon-image(-static)` → `hero-image(-static)`, keyframes `hero-image-cycle`. The unused Coming Soon rules (page, main, panel, kicker, lead, copy, register, cta, qr, details, footer, actions) were deleted. `qrcode` and `@types/qrcode` were removed; `lucide-react` stays (used by the donate and news pages).
- AC1 says Home renders "hero images". In the T011 content, Home uses the map hero with no photos, so the photo crossfade was checked on `/about`, which uses the Coming Soon background images.
- The `coming-soon` global's live preview config was removed along with hiding it; it has no schema effect.
- `/api/health` lives at `src/app/api/health/route.ts`. Static segments take precedence over Payload's `/api/[...slug]`.
- Human steps (not in the repo): in Vercel, confirm the production branch is `main` and leave `SITE_LIVE` unset for Production; in Supabase, turn on automatic RLS for new tables.
